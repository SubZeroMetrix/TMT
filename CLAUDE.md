enablement for service businesses, contractors as the beachhead. Sells outcomes and judgment,
**never software builds**.

## Evidence labelling — required in any audit, teardown, or proposal

Every material claim carries its provenance. Never let an inference read as a fact.

`OBSERVED` verified directly (site, CRM, analytics, reviews, SERP) · `CALCULATED` derived from
verified inputs, show the arithmetic · `INFERRED` reasonable but unproven, say so · `UNKNOWN`
evidence unavailable — name it rather than filling the gap · `RECOMMENDED` TMT's proposed action.

If a capability needed for a section is unavailable, mark that section `PENDING <capability>` and
deliver everything else. Never stall a whole deliverable on one missing input, and never
substitute a guess for it.

## Project rules

- **Claims require evidence.** Never invent a metric, testimonial, case study, or client name — use
  `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]`. There are no published case studies yet; do not imply otherwise.
- **No vendor-partnership claims.** TMT has no vendor relationships and takes no commission.
  "Vendor-neutral" is accurate; "participates in partner programs" is false and has shipped once
  before. Site copy, the vendor-payment FAQ, and `llms.txt` change together, disclosure first.
- **Everything is local.** St. Petersburg is home base, Tampa is target two. Never write
  nationally-framed TMT content. Keep `lib/seo/geo.ts` and the brain's city list in sync.
- **NAP exactly, everywhere:** The Modern Trades Mentor LLC · 727-600-3425 ·
  PO Box 66093, St. Petersburg, FL 33767
- **Never soften the free-visit boundary.** The Shop Visit excludes solution design, vendor
  comparison, and any written roadmap. That limit is what stops the diagnosis being given away.
- The **$1,500** audit price is a **founding-client price for the first three engagements** — the
  "founding" framing must travel with the number everywhere it appears.
- **No autonomous sending, posting, or outreach.** Draft only; the owner sends.
- Every recommendation passes NIST AI RMF — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. If it fails one, it does
  not go in a roadmap or on the site.
- **Never publicly name a business in a teardown.** Anonymize by trade and market size.
- Banned words: leverage, synergy, transform, empower, solutions, seamless, cutting-edge,
  revolutionize, unlock, AI-powered, robust, streamline. Banned jargon: LLM, RAG, agentic, prompt
  engineering, model. Use their units: calls, trucks, jobs, tickets, callbacks, close rate, drive
  time, dispatch, estimate, invoice, season, techs, the office.
