# TMT — First 90 Days Scorecard

Internal. Not a public page — targets on a public site read as either bragging or as promises.

**The goal is proof, not scale.** Three paid audits with measured outcomes beats thirty free visits
and a bigger website.

Start date: `[FILL IN]` · Review dates: 30 / 60 / 90 days

---

## Targets

These are operating targets, not forecasts and not promises to anyone.

| Outcome | Target | Actual |
|---|---:|---:|
| Qualified shop visits held | 10 | |
| Paid audits sold | 3 | |
| Paid implementation projects | 1–2 | |
| Measurable case studies | 2 | |
| Genuine Google reviews | 3 | |
| Local partnerships / referral sources | 3 | |

"Qualified" means the fit criteria were met before the drive: 5–30 employees, a real office-to-field
workflow, existing pain in follow-up / scheduling / quoting / CRM / dispatch / reporting / admin,
and the decision-maker present.

---

## Decision gates

Check these at the gate, not continuously. The point is to change one thing at a time based on
evidence rather than react to every slow week.

**After 10 qualified visits → fewer than 3 paid audits sold**
The problem is qualification, audience, or the offer. **It is not the website.** Look at who is
turning up, whether the decision-maker attends, and whether the audit is being presented as a
priced product with a defined deliverable or as a vague next step.

**After 3 audits → no implementation sold**
The audit is too vague, not tied to money or time, or no defined next step is being asked for.
Fix the deliverable before selling more audits.

**After 2 implementations → no measurable before/after result**
Narrow the use cases and improve delivery before chasing more leads. Without a measured result
there is no case study, and without case studies the next ten sales are just as hard as the last.

---

## The three channels, and nothing else, for 90 days

1. **Direct local relationships** — former operations contacts, contractors, suppliers, facilities
   connections, local trade groups. This is the fastest path to the first three audits.
2. **Google local discovery** — verified service-area Business Profile, complete services list,
   authentic job-site and process photos, real reviews.
3. **Proof-led content** — one useful field observation per week. Not AI news.

Adding a fourth channel before these three are working is how the 90 days gets wasted.

---

## What does not count as progress

- More website pages
- More free visits that were never qualified
- Social followers
- Anything that cannot be tied to a booked visit, a sold audit, or a measured result
