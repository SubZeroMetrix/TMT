# TMT — Reconciled 90-Day Operating Plan

Internal only. Not a public page. Supersedes the seasonality-phased channel plan from the
pasted external documents — verification found that argument unsupported (see the plan
file's verification ledger, 2026-08). Everything else verified as true from those
documents is retained here, reconciled against the actual repo and Richard's own prior
docs (`90-day-scorecard.md`, `proof-loop.md`, `highlevel-automations.md`).

## North star

Founder-led contractor operations-improvement practice. Diagnose (audit) → fix (sprint) →
maintain (partnership). AI/automation/CRM are tools used inside that, not the category —
this is a repositioning of what TMT already is, not a rebuild.

## ICP — unchanged

5–30 employees, owner-led, real office-to-field workflow. Two external documents disagreed
with each other on widening this to 10–75; resolved by keeping the current live language
and deciding with real audit data after ~10 completed audits, not before.

## Channels — HVAC-focused, without narrowing the site

- **Direct outreach** — Tampa Bay HVAC first, then plumbing/electrical. Other trades
  remain live on the site for inbound/SEO; they are simply not the outbound focus yet.
- **RACCA** — verified real: Tampa-based, founded 1949, serves exactly Pinellas/
  Hillsborough/Pasco, offers CE and apprenticeship programs. Approach as education, not
  sponsorship. Candidate session title: "Seven Operating Leaks Hiding in a Modern HVAC
  Company."
- Everything else (PHCC, TBBA, podcast guesting, paid ads, content engines) stays out of
  the critical path for 90 days — optionality, not priority.

## Capacity ceiling — the constraint that governs how much gets sold

Roughly 4 ongoing partnership clients + 1 active sprint + occasional audits. Two days a
week protected for business development even when delivery gets busy — the pipeline
empties if all five days go to client work. Delivery is Richard-plus-vetted-specialists
once proven, not Richard-does-everything-forever; this is reflected in the new sprint
page copy.

## Scoreboard — six numbers, checked weekly

Qualified conversations · shop visits · audits sold · sprints sold · cash collected ·
qualified pipeline $. Everything else is secondary for the first 90 days.

## Day 30 / Day 90 gates

Day 30: 30 real conversations, target 3 paid audits. Zero despite 30 real conversations →
fix the offer, not the website.

Day 90: 75–100 conversations, 5–7 audits, 2+ sprints, 1 partnership would be excellent, 2
case studies, $12K–$25K collected as the validation floor. The real question at Day 90 is
not the revenue number — it's whether Tampa Bay HVAC owners will pay TMT to diagnose and
fix operating problems. If yes, continue. If audits sell but nothing converts further, the
audit isn't exposing enough economic pain — fix that before selling more of them.

## Explicitly not part of this 90 days

No SaaS product, no second brand, no national ads, no hiring, no broad AI-consulting
campaign, no long nurture sequences (nothing to nurture with yet — zero case studies), no
broad SMS marketing (10DLC status unconfirmed — verify before ever revisiting), no
automated or incentivized review requests (permanent, not time-limited — see
`proof-loop.md`).
